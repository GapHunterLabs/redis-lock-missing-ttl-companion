Reports a manual Redis lock taken with SET ... NX (Jedis SetParams.nx() or Lettuce SetArgs.Builder.nx()) with no EX or PX expiry on the same call.

If the process holding the lock crashes before releasing it, the lock never expires and blocks every other caller. Redisson RLock.lock() without a lease time is not reported: its watchdog renews the lock while the holder is alive and lets it expire if the holder dies.
